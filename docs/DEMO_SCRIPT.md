# ClaimBack — 90-Second Demo

## Opening

> A merchant can make a sale perfectly and still lose money before the product even reaches the shelf.

> Suppliers can send fewer units, change an agreed rate, miss a scheme, or promise a credit that never appears. Small merchants track this through paper, WhatsApp and memory.

> ClaimBack is their AI Margin Protector.

## Scene 1 — dashboard

Open **Sharma Medical**.

Show:

- Margin protected: ₹10,860
- Pending recovery: ₹1,560
- Open claims: 3

Click **Receive Stock**.

## Scene 2 — invoice

Upload demo invoice.

Visible action:

`✓ Invoice understood`

Briefly show extracted lines.

## Scene 3 — supplier promise

Upload/show:

> 50 boxes, ₹428, 10+1 scheme.

Visible action:

`✓ Supplier promise matched`

## Scene 4 — actual delivery

Say or type:

> 48 boxes aaye, aur do damaged hain.

ClaimBack structures the receiving record.

## Scene 5 — source-grounded discrepancy

Show discrepancy cards with:

- amount;
- promised source;
- billed source;
- received source;
- deterministic calculation.

Say:

> AI understands the invoice, supplier message and merchant's Hinglish. The rupee calculation is deterministic code.

## Scene 6 — action

Potential recovery appears.

Merchant clicks:

**Approve & Send Claim**

Timeline:

- ✓ Evidence compiled
- ✓ Claim created
- ✓ Supplier contacted

## Scene 7 — supplier response

Trigger simulator.

Supplier says:

> Shortage approved. Rate difference not approved. Credit next invoice.

ClaimBack parses accepted/rejected portions, uses evidence if needed, and keeps the obligation open.

## Scene 8 — hero differentiator

Say:

> The supplier saying “next invoice mein adjust kar denge” is not recovery. ClaimBack remembers that obligation and verifies the next invoice.

Show:

`AWAITING RECOVERY — ₹X outstanding`

## Scene 9 — verification

Upload corrected invoice/credit note.

ClaimBack checks it against the open obligation.

Final:

**₹X RECOVERED ✓**

State:

**RESOLVED**

Dashboard margin protected increases.

## Scene 10 — growth layer

Very briefly show supplier intelligence:

- discrepancy rate;
- claimed/recovered;
- recovery speed.

## Closing

> ClaimBack doesn't just tell the merchant what went wrong. It remembers what was promised, takes the recovery workflow forward, and verifies that the money or stock actually came back.
