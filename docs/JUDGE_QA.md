# ClaimBack Judge Q&A

## What is ClaimBack?

ClaimBack is an AI Margin Protector for small merchants.

It remembers what suppliers promised, compares that with the invoice and actual delivery, detects supported leakage, creates evidence, follows the claim, and verifies that the merchant actually received the promised recovery.

## How is this Merchant Growth AI?

Growth is not only higher sales.

A merchant can increase revenue and still lose profit through short deliveries, wrong purchase rates, missing schemes, damaged goods, and forgotten credits.

ClaimBack protects gross margin and working capital.

## Why AI?

Merchant purchasing evidence is messy:

- paper invoices;
- screenshots;
- Hinglish/local-language speech;
- informal scheme messages;
- supplier replies;
- inconsistent product names.

AI structures the evidence.

Deterministic software handles money and workflow invariants.

## Isn't this just OCR?

No.

OCR tells us what one document says.

ClaimBack links evidence across time:

supplier promise  
+ invoice  
+ receiving statement  
+ supplier response  
+ future credit.

Persistent financial memory is the differentiator.

## How do you prevent false claims?

Three safeguards:

1. every discrepancy is source-grounded;
2. final money is deterministic;
3. a clean delivery produces `No claim required`.

Merchant approval is required before sending.

## Why not a normal rules engine?

Rules work after data is structured.

The target merchant often has unstructured evidence across photos, messages, and speech.

AI performs the structuring and semantic matching.

## Why not just call the supplier?

For one obvious missing box, the merchant can.

ClaimBack becomes more valuable when:

- many line items exist;
- rate/scheme promises live in messages;
- recovery happens later;
- multiple cases remain open;
- repeated follow-up is needed;
- supplier reliability matters over time.

## What is autonomous?

After evidence exists, ClaimBack can:

- reconcile;
- prepare the evidence packet;
- create a claim;
- send after approval;
- interpret supplier response;
- preserve unresolved obligations;
- check a later credit/replacement;
- resolve only when verified.

It does not merely answer a question.

## Why merchant approval?

A supplier-facing financial claim is a meaningful external action.

The merchant stays in control while the surrounding work is automated.

## What is simulated?

- supplier behavior/timing.

Real:

- AI extraction;
- structured evidence;
- deterministic reconciliation;
- state transitions;
- agent tools;
- claim generation;
- supplier-response interpretation;
- future-credit verification.

## Why Paytm if the MVP is standalone?

The challenge asks teams to think beyond payments.

ClaimBack extends the merchant relationship into purchasing-margin protection.

A future Paytm product could use:

- Paytm for Business as camera/workflow surface;
- a Soundbox-like interface for reminders/voice.

The MVP does not pretend private Paytm integrations exist.

## What is the moat?

Not simply “AI claims.”

The differentiation is:

**camera/voice-first receiving + source-grounded evidence + persistent supplier financial memory + closed-loop recovery for merchants without structured procurement software.**

Over time the data compounds into supplier intelligence.

## What is measurable?

Primary:

**₹ recovered / margin protected**

Not chatbot engagement.
