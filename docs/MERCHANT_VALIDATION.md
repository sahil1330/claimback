# Merchant Validation — Fast Field Guide

## Purpose

The largest unresolved product risk is whether the target merchant experiences supplier leakage and delayed-credit follow-up often enough to care.

Speak to 3–5 target merchants if possible.

Prioritize:

- pharmacies;
- FMCG-heavy retailers;
- wholesalers;
- cosmetics shops;
- electronics/accessories stores.

## Questions

Ask naturally:

1. Do deliveries ever arrive short?
2. Do suppliers ever bill a different rate than agreed?
3. Are free units/schemes ever missing?
4. What happens with damaged stock?
5. Do suppliers say they will adjust something in a later invoice?
6. How do you remember those promised credits?
7. Do you ever forget to check whether the credit actually came?
8. How do you currently track this?
9. Roughly how often does a discrepancy happen?
10. Would taking an invoice photo and speaking what arrived be easier than the current process?

## Strong signals

Stories like:

- “scheme ka issue hota hai”
- “credit note reh jaata hai”
- “rate galat lagta hai”
- “damage claim follow-up karna padta hai”
- “next bill mein adjust bolte hain”
- “baad mein yaad nahi rehta”

## Weak signal

If most merchants say:

> Delivery boy fixes everything immediately and later credits are never a problem.

then the need is weak for that segment.

Do not hide that result.

## Pitch rule

Use only validation that actually happened.

Do not invent merchant counts or quotes.

Anonymize unless explicit attribution permission exists.
